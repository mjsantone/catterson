Standfirst + The Gist

Reserves by Wren Halloway June 2026 · 14 min read

A third of humanity can no longer see the Milky Way from where they sleep. The world's last dark places are being mapped, ranked, and defended as infrastructure.

The Gist

In short

  • Darkness is now a measured resource. Reserves are certified on a 0–22 sky-brightness scale.
  • The loss is accelerating. Artificial brightness is climbing 9.6% a year.
  • Protection works, cheaply. Shielded lighting cuts skyglow without cutting safety.

07 / Sequence

Chronology stays in normal flow

Vertical timeline

Separate the useful evidence from the material that merely proves the research happened.

Structure
Assign editorial weight

Decide what opens, what interrupts, what supports, and what belongs in the source line.

08 / Rules

The system is mostly what it refuses to do

01One argument per page

Every component must clarify, support, or pace that argument.

02Evidence earns scale

A number becomes large because it turns the story, not because a layout needs a hero.

03Color works in pairs

Light surfaces carry dark text. Mid tones are reserved for marks and data.

04Variation stays bounded

The subject can change completely while the type, measure, spacing, and caption logic hold.
